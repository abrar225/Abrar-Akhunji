# Browser critique of the five portfolio versions

The baseline is the four commits in the [version manifest](../../versions/manifest.json) and the Signal Field build at `abb86ca`. Chromium 158 ran the local production files at 1440 × 900 with a mouse and 390 × 844 with emulated touch. Home recordings use wheel and touch input. Each direct URL has viewport captures at the top, midpoint, and end, with section-specific frames where a scroll percentage would miss a key scene. The user-supplied Home, Work, and About screenshots were unavailable. All images here came from this audit. [Desktop comparison](evidence/baseline/desktop-comparison.jpg) · [Mobile comparison](evidence/baseline/mobile-comparison.jpg) · [Capture index](evidence/baseline/README.md)

The UI/UX review emphasizes the path from identity to work evidence and contact. The motion review follows objects and changes in pace across that path. The frontend review checks navigation, layout geometry, select controls, and the cost of the initial screen. Startup samples below are single, unthrottled loopback observations. JavaScript sizes are raw same-origin response bodies recorded before scrolling. The figures establish rough load differences across these runs. Production mobile lab targets and real-device behavior remain unverified. ## V1 · Serif Studio

**Concept, hierarchy, typography and color · Evolve it.** The centered “Engineer of Intelligent Systems” headline, violet script accent and dark open space make the hero recognizable. The name is reduced to the small ABRAR. wordmark and the headline leaves the work domain broad. Keep the type contrast as emphasis on a concrete engineering claim. [Desktop Home, arrival](evidence/baseline/v1/desktop/home/00.jpg) · [Mobile Home, arrival](evidence/baseline/v1/mobile/home/00.jpg)

**Layout and components · Keep the principle.** The wide work sequence places a real interface beside a concise description, technical bullets, stack and demo/source. At every sampled width the useful unit is the project with evidence beside it. The desktop composition uses a right-cropped screenshot and peeking adjacent cards, while the phone turns projects into stacked modules. Both require considerable travel after the About section. [Desktop Home, work](evidence/baseline/v1/desktop/home/50.jpg) · [Mobile Home, work](evidence/baseline/v1/mobile/home/50.jpg)

**Motion and transitions · Never repeat it.** In the desktop recording, a blurred About panel clears before the visitor reaches the horizontally staged cards. The animated dark field and hover cursor supply atmosphere without giving the next section a common object to follow. On the phone, the sequence still changes from type to a code-styled panel to a photo to cards. The small dock remains fixed throughout, so it takes attention even through calm sections. [Desktop Home recording](evidence/baseline/recordings/v1-desktop.webm) · [Mobile Home recording](evidence/baseline/recordings/v1-mobile.webm)

**Mobile, accessibility and recruiter clarity · Evolve it.** Content fits at 390 px in the sampled viewport. Work and résumé rely on compact dock items or a long scroll. “Hire Me” has a clear text label, while several neighboring controls read mainly as glyphs. The archive injects its own disclosure and retired-assistant line, which overlap the mobile navigation area and are archive adaptations. Keep a short plain-language work action and enough room for labeled first-screen routes. [Mobile Home, arrival](evidence/baseline/v1/mobile/home/00.jpg) · [Mobile Home, end](evidence/baseline/v1/mobile/home/100.jpg)

**Performance · Evolve it.** The desktop arrival fetched about 0.98 MB of JavaScript in this run, compared with 0.50 MB on touch. The decorative desktop background accounts for the larger initial payload. A responsive fallback preserves the visual idea while keeping the first actions independent of decorative code. The archive disables the original timed entrance, so its timing is outside this critique. [Home observations, desktop](evidence/baseline/v1/desktop/home/observed.json) · [Home observations, mobile](evidence/baseline/v1/mobile/home/observed.json)

## V2 · Terminal Pulse

**Concept, hierarchy, typography and color · Evolve it.** Warm orange and near-black, the large left-aligned headline, code textures and monospace metadata create a coherent technical mood. The opening still leads with the broad “Engineer of Intelligent Systems” line. Its short description, open-to-work status and identity are visually quieter than the statement. On phone the heading and short copy are present, with a compact dock carrying the primary actions. [Desktop Home, arrival](evidence/baseline/v2/desktop/home/00.jpg) · [Mobile Home, arrival](evidence/baseline/v2/mobile/home/00.jpg)

**Layout and components · Keep the principle.** The split work entries give prose and previews different jobs. Terminal-shaped project evidence fits FixO CLI. Writing has its own route, prominent titles, dates, read times and a visible alternative index mode. The contrast between an expressive portfolio and a quiet destination for articles is worth keeping. The archived live assistant and its shortcut are retired, so their original behavior is unverified. [Desktop Home, work](evidence/baseline/v2/desktop/home/50.jpg) · [Writing](evidence/baseline/v2/desktop/blog/00.jpg) · [Article](evidence/baseline/v2/desktop/blog--2026-07-20-ponytail-ai-agent-lazy-senior-dev/00.jpg)

**Motion and transitions · Never repeat it.** Large kinetic words enter, shift, then hand off to the projects. On desktop the “SENSE / LEARN / REASON / BUILD” passage occupies several moments of the recording before the relevant work. The motion is distinctive and cinematic. A future interlude should advance an observable element of the engineering story at each step. [Desktop Home recording](evidence/baseline/recordings/v2-desktop.webm) · [Mobile Home recording](evidence/baseline/recordings/v2-mobile.webm)

**Mobile, accessibility and recruiter clarity · Evolve it.** The phone gives the photograph and each project a distinct vertical beat. In the dark writing index, secondary labels are visually quiet beside bright titles and orange tags, and the retired archive notice and dock compete at the viewport bottom. A direct portfolio return link remains usable. Give technical metadata and footer links a legible floor in both themes. [Mobile Home, work](evidence/baseline/v2/mobile/home/50.jpg) · [Mobile Writing](evidence/baseline/v2/mobile/blog/00.jpg)

**Performance · Evolve it.** The arrival requested about 1.09 MB of JavaScript desktop and 0.61 MB mobile. Desktop startup recorded roughly 0.048 cumulative layout shift in this run. Reserve stable space for the hero and load any cinematic interlude after the initial content. ## V3 · Halide Signal

**Concept, hierarchy, typography and color · Keep the principle.** Cyan optical highlights tie the italic serif word “Intelligent,” the dark technical imagery and the ASCII signal together. The arrival includes dedicated work, résumé and contact actions. On mobile the actions sit above the animating graphic, which keeps the visual signature from taking the space needed to choose a destination. [Desktop Home, arrival](evidence/baseline/v3/desktop/home/00.jpg) · [Mobile Home, arrival](evidence/baseline/v3/mobile/home/00.jpg)

**Layout and components · Evolve it.** The lead project has a full split feature, further projects appear as paired studies, and the skills and experience chapters compress into different structures. Writing has list/tech tree modes and numerous topic filters. On the phone the filter area takes most of the first Writing viewport. Keep the fresh content hierarchy and reduce the decision load before the first readable entry. [Desktop Home recording](evidence/baseline/recordings/v3-desktop.webm) · [Mobile Writing](evidence/baseline/v3/mobile/blog/00.jpg) · [Mobile Hire](evidence/baseline/v3/mobile/hire/00.jpg)

**Motion and transitions · Evolve it.** The ASCII signal is recognizable at the hero and the activity at the footer forms a related cyan field. Between the two the visual through-line largely yields to project and record modules. Borrow the principle of a recurring signal, then make its intermediate states part of the story. [Desktop Home, arrival](evidence/baseline/v3/desktop/home/00.jpg) · [Desktop Home, end](evidence/baseline/v3/desktop/home/100.jpg)

**Mobile, accessibility and recruiter clarity · Evolve it.** On touch the work links, categories and feature details remain text. The first screen still asks a recruiter to translate the large positioning line and the small wordmark. Light primary actions hold their legibility against the dark canvas, while muted subtitles and tags have less visual separation. Keep a clear shortcut out of the expressive hero and lead with name and specific domain. [Mobile Home, arrival](evidence/baseline/v3/mobile/home/00.jpg) · [Mobile Home recording](evidence/baseline/recordings/v3-mobile.webm)

## V4 · Build Room

**Concept, hierarchy, typography and color · Keep the principle.** Three actual projects live on one miniature workbench. Their physical shapes, cream stock, cobalt accents and restrained handwriting explain a maker’s story through individual artifacts. Role, eligibility for opportunities, location and action links are visible on desktop. The large “Zero to built.” occupies the display role, while the name and professional role are in the byline. [Desktop Home, arrival](evidence/baseline/v4/desktop/home/00.jpg) · [Mobile Home, arrival](evidence/baseline/v4/mobile/home/00.jpg)

**Layout variety and components · Keep the principle.** The opening type/scene split gives way to a friction brief, a stage with selectors, a cutaway with a build receipt, a credentials area and an end scene. The 3D bench, work pages and project studies have different roles. A real work sample is reachable from the same home scene through the product summary. The browser route inventory also shows the conventional two-column work index and case-study contents rail. [Desktop workbench](evidence/baseline/v4/desktop/home/work.jpg) · [Desktop cutaway](evidence/baseline/v4/desktop/home/engineering.jpg) · [Desktop page archetypes](evidence/baseline/v4-route-archetypes.jpg)

**Motion and transitions · Evolve it.** The same three objects change camera position and relationship as the visitor moves from introduction to work. The mobile reading order gives the friction brief and product summaries space alongside the lighter opening poster. “Explore in 3D” supplies an explicit entered state, the drag changes the camera position, and Escape returns to the scroll flow. ## Current V5 · Signal Field

**Concept, hierarchy, typography and color · Keep the principle.** The cobalt graph field and halftone portrait frame make the arrival recognizable. The full name, plain-language engineering domains, Ahmedabad, availability, Work and Résumé are all visible on phone. Contact is visible in the open mobile menu. The cobalt atmosphere becomes a persistent narrow header edge across much of the reading journey, while cream, green, tan, navy and pink take over as the scenes change. Preserve the explicit hero information and make the blue field participate in the transitions. ## Comparison

| Version | Organizing idea and medium | Main passage | Recruiter path | Avoid carrying over |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| V1 | Centered violet identity, ornamental WebGL field | Blur reveal into a horizontal project shelf that becomes a stack | Compact dock, deep project evidence and contact finale | Small icon-first shortcuts and a long lead-in to projects |
| V2 | Ember-colored terminal editorial | Full-width kinetic verbs separate the intro from work | Hire action and project demos, plus a standalone writing index | Interlude without progress or a connection to a named build |
| V3 | Optical typography and ASCII signals | ASCII hero to split feature, study grid and a river finale | Visible work, résumé and contact actions at arrival | Repeated ASCII visual for its own sake and dense phone filter piles |
| V4 | Working build room with props that represent real projects | One camera and set of objects connect the intro, brief, workbench and architecture cutaway | Work, project, case-study and contact routes with an optional 3D path | Pinned workshop, overlapping navigation layers and large startup cost |
| Current V5 | Cobalt print framing around a collage and real screenshots | Color changes and edge seams with repeated entrance grammar | Explicit professional identity and direct project URLs | Paper-edge repetition, generic masthead changes and stacked replies |

## What the new V5 has to beat

V4 made the product itself the recurring object. Its desktop arrival and workbench show the same three physical builds, and the phone carries the visual logic through a distinct layout. V5 must connect a real engineering decision to its consequence across multiple different structures. The same work, résumé and contact paths need to stay visible and quick during that motion. Each additional pointer, touch or scroll interaction should help a visitor inspect how a specific piece of the software works. [V4 desktop arrival](evidence/baseline/v4/desktop/home/00.jpg) · [V4 desktop workbench](evidence/baseline/v4/desktop/home/work.jpg) · [V4 mobile workbench](evidence/baseline/v4/mobile/home/work.jpg)

V4 also provides a grounded progression from friction to product, architecture and delivery evidence. Current V5 preserved the project URLs and real imagery. A new direction earns its place if the public Home demonstrates that progression without turning the next page into a second tour. Separate visual cadence should make it immediately clear when the visitor has gone from inspection to reading or contact.
