# Flow Music production briefs

## Background music — Little Useful Things

Create a calm, quietly joyful instrumental loop for a creative software engineer's portfolio. Warm felt piano, rounded marimba, airy soft synth pads, subtle wooden percussion, 78 BPM, D major pentatonic. Spacious and welcoming, gently curious, no vocals, no dramatic build, no harsh highs or heavy bass. Seamless 60-second loop with a soft tail. Keep space for short interface sound effects. Title: Little Useful Things.

## FX / instruments — The Sound Room

Build a small downloadable interface sound kit for the same portfolio in D major pentatonic. Warm, tactile and precise. Soft mallet attacks, gentle rounded glass, tiny wooden clicks and airy transitions. No harsh digital bleeps. The music should remain the quiet background. Generate/export isolated WAVs with short attack/release envelopes and silence at their ends. Each filename must match its role:

`hover` (35 ms, felt tap), `click` (70 ms, wood and soft glass), `select` (110 ms, two warm notes), `section` (220 ms, airy upward turn), `chapter` (450 ms, soft time-skip sweep), `building` (45–70 ms, a single rounded marimba pluck suitable for pitch variation), `replay` (320 ms, a gentle rewind then lift), `city-complete` (650 ms, a restrained D major pentatonic cadence), `scatter` (250 ms, fine paper/granular dispersion), `reform` (400 ms, grains settling into a soft chord), `creature` (90 ms, a curious little wooden chirp), `ripple` (280 ms, small water ring), `breach` (350 ms, soft water lift), `splash` (500 ms, a small natural water landing).

Export real generated files from Flow Music. Record the Flow session/space URLs, download paths, timestamps and hashes in the final provenance manifest. Do not replace missing output with files presented as Flow-generated.
